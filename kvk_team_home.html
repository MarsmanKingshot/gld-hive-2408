Everyone not in a team

If you are not on your alliance's roster for Saturday, or your troops are T8 or below, your job is to be at home, shielded, and healing others. Their players can enter 2408 from 10:00 to 12:00 and from 17:00 to 22:00 UTC and will look for unshielded cities. Nobody has to defend our castle: as attacker, it cannot be attacked.

Shields. Shield on except while your own attack march is out. Reinforcing keeps your shield; launching a rally drops the lead's; joining one does not drop yours. An enemy march coming at you: if your own attack marches are home, shield now and it turns back. Your city stands in their kingdom all day.
