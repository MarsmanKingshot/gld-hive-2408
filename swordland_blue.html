Our buildings on this side

At 0:00: Sanctum, Spawn Abbey, Corner Abbey, Stables; the Bell Tower follows as the Spawn Abbey team's second job once its Abbey is held; then the Shrine at 15:00.

Stables. The Stables is on the RIGHT half, their side, at the bottom corner, two screens from our Corner Abbey. We take it anyway: it halves the teleport cooldown for whoever holds it, and we do not want them to have it. The Stables team teleports to the bottom corner beside the Corner Abbey team, and the two teams cover each other.

Bell Tower. The Bell Tower is ours too, at the top corner, next to the enemy Corner Abbey and their striker. It is the Spawn Abbey team's second job: once a joiner march sits in the Spawn Abbey, the anchor and looter teleport to the Bell Tower and take it. If it falls, nobody goes back for it before 45:00.

Corner Abbey is the Abbey at the far corner of the left half, the bottom of the map. Spawn Abbey is the one right beside our spawn diamond. Check the map before you pin; both are called Abbey in the game.

Marches: everyone has four to six, so none sits idle

Every march that is not holding something should be out collecting. Trains first, Undercellars second, always on our side of the line, never by teleport. A gathering march carries the minimum number of troops the game lets you send, five. That is enough to empty a node, and losing five troops costs nothing, so nobody protects a gathering march and nobody waits for it. Never send more than the minimum: the troops are needed in the buildings.

Topping up garrisons. Troops injured in a building go to the field infirmary on their own, so the garrison simply shrinks after every hit. Nothing is swapped and nothing is recalled: when the lead calls REINFORCE, you send another march and the game takes whatever fits under your own cap in that building. Heal in the field infirmary, which is free, and send again. The lead checks the troop count in the building every five minutes and after every rally against it.

Undercellars by team. Six nodes on our side, three per cluster. They appear twice, at 15:00 and again at 20:00. The Sanctum team owns the cluster nearest the Sanctum and the Spawn Abbey team the cluster nearest the Spawn Abbey. They send first, one march per node. Everyone else sends spare marches to a node only if it is still empty; a node already being gathered is left alone until the nodes appear again at 20:00.

When it goes wrong: the lead decides, by these rules

No lead waits for a call. Each situation below has a fixed answer, so every team reacts the same way without asking.

Our building is lost.

  1. Trains first, always. The moment it flips, the looter and every spare march go for the Baggage Trains it drops. That is half its bank and it is on the ground for anyone.
  2. Retake during their capture. Their capture takes three minutes (one and a half if they hold the Bell Tower). In that window the striker burns the enemy city parked beside the building, never the building: a burned anchor is sent to spawn and their march leaves the building. The anchor's main march then goes back in and the joiners' free marches follow with speedups.
  3. Lost twice, or held by more than we can match: stop feeding it. The lead posts "LOST building" in alliance chat. The anchor and striker stay parked one screen away. Joiners' free marches go to the nearest home building in this order: Sanctum, Corner Abbey, Spawn Abbey, Stables. The striker joins the nearest team's rallies.
  4. Take it back when they leave it. A strong enemy can hold one, maybe two buildings. Every time their anchor teleports away, the garrison goes with them. The team watches the troop count above the building and goes back in the moment it drops, with spare marches first and the anchor's march once it is ours.
  5. Never empty a second building to retake the first. A building lost is 600 or 1,200 a minute; two buildings lost is the match.

Our anchor is burned. Their march leaves the building and they are in spawn for twelve minutes. The striker's main march goes into the building at once as stand-in anchor, the joiners top up, and the burned anchor returns to one screen from the building and re-enters when the stand-in says the building is full. If the striker is also gone, the looter's main march holds it.

A rally is coming at our building. The lead calls REINFORCE; every free march goes in with speedups. The striker burns the rally leader's city if it is parked within one screen, which breaks the rally. Nobody leaves the building to counter-attack.

Decisions, in one list

  1. One building per team, pinned by the lead before 0:00. No extra building before 45:00, however empty it looks.
  2. The Stables always gets a team. The Bell Tower is a second job, never a reason to leave an Abbey: an Abbey is worth two and a half buff buildings.
  3. Park one screen from your building, toward our spawn. Never beside the building, never beside a teammate.
  4. Rallies are five minutes when the joiners are in the safe zone, one minute only when the four named joiners are within one screen. Join only your own team's rallies. No gems to reach a rally.
  5. Rally enemy cities beside a building, never the building, never alone.
  6. Anchors never teleport while their march is in a building. Teleporting pulls your troops out of everything.
  7. Free speedups on every reinforcement march. They refill one a minute.
  8. No idle marches. Anything not holding a building or a city is out for trains or Undercellars on our side of the line, the minimum of five troops per node, never more. Nobody ever teleports for one.
  9. If your city is about to be hit, send everything out and recall at the last second. Being burned costs no troops, only twelve minutes.
  10. Team chat only. Alliance chat carries the roll call, the score, "REINFORCE building" and "PLAN B". Nothing else.
