When it goes wrong: the lead decides, by these rules

Our building is lost

  • Trains first, always. The moment it flips, the looter and every spare march go for the Baggage Trains it drops. That is half its bank, on the ground for anyone.
  • Retake during their capture. Their capture takes three minutes (one and a half if they hold the Bell Tower). In that window the striker burns the enemy city parked beside the building, never the building: a burned anchor is sent to spawn and their march leaves the building. The anchor's main march then goes back in and the joiners' free marches follow with speedups.
  • Lost twice, or held by more than we can match: stop feeding it. The lead posts "LOST building" in alliance chat. The anchor and striker stay parked one screen away. Joiners' free marches go to the nearest home building in this order: Sanctum, Corner Abbey, Spawn Abbey, Stables. The striker joins the nearest team's rallies.
  • Take it back when they leave it. Every time their anchor teleports away, the garrison goes with them. Watch the troop count above the building and go back in the moment it drops: spare marches first, the anchor's march once it is ours.
  • Never empty a second building to retake the first. One building lost is 600 or 1,200 a minute; two lost is the match.

Our anchor is burned

Their march leaves the building and the anchor is in spawn for twelve minutes. The striker's main march goes into the building at once as stand-in anchor, the joiners top up, and the burned anchor comes back to one screen from the building and re-enters when the stand-in says it is full. If the striker is also gone, the looter's main march holds it.

A rally is coming at our building

The lead calls REINFORCE; every free march goes in with speedups. The striker burns the rally leader's city if it is parked within one screen. Nobody leaves the building to counter-attack.

An enemy building next to ours empties

Their anchor teleported and the count above it dropped to one march or none: spare marches only go in, never the anchor's or the striker's main march. If it holds, the lead names a sub-anchor from the joiners. Anything garrisoned on their side of the line is left alone before 50:00.

At 45:00

The Sanctum lead posts both scores. Ahead by more than 30,000: nobody attacks anything, every garrison is topped up, looters watch for flips. Behind by less than 30,000: at 50:00 two rallies ten seconds apart on the enemy building with the biggest bank, named by the Shrine lead; the strikers of the Stables and Corner Abbey teams join, every looter marches on the trains, home anchors never move. Behind by more than 60,000 against a clearly stronger alliance: every team holds its building for the occupation bonus and sends every spare march collecting for personal points.

Rules everyone keeps

  • One building per team, pinned by the lead before 0:00. No extra building before 45:00, however empty it looks.
  • Park one screen from your building, toward our spawn. Never beside the building, never beside a teammate.
  • Anchors never teleport while their march is in a building. Teleporting pulls your troops out of everything.
  • Rally enemy cities beside a building, never the building, never alone. Join only your own team's rallies. No gems to reach a rally.
  • Free speedups on every reinforcement march. They refill one a minute.
  • No idle marches. Anything not holding a building or a city is out for trains or Undercellars on our side of the line, the minimum of five troops per node, never more. Nobody ever teleports for one.
  • If your city is about to be hit, send everything out and recall at the last second. Being burned costs no troops, only twelve minutes.
  • Team chat only. Alliance chat carries the roll call, the score, "REINFORCE building", "LOST building" and "PLAN B". Nothing else.
