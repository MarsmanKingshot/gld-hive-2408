How we win

Hold the castle continuously for 2½ hours as a kingdom. If neither kingdom does, the alliance with the longest total occupation decides the winning kingdom. Keep castle time in GLD where practical.

Count from the actual capture: 12:03 gives a possible win at 14:33. A loss resets that continuous hold, but a later hold can still win if enough time remains. Same-kingdom turrets do not fire on our castle garrison and provide up to +20% squad Lethality.

Battle rules and team choices

  • Use the lead’s preset and send only on a call. Only members of the holding alliance can directly reinforce its structure.
  • Keep Infirmary space available. City attacks have different casualty rules from castle fights; do not treat them as loss-free.
  • City attacks before 17:00 are an optional team tactic, subject to current terms and explicit target approval. Points fighting does not first unlock at 17:00.
  • For city-battle points, check the three-level Town Center range, including Truegold stars. Rally scoring uses the captain’s level; reinforced targets use the assisted governor’s level.
  • A support alliance that retakes a turret must fill it with its own members. Agree who holds it before sending troops.
