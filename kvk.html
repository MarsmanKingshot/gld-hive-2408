T8 and below do not travel: garrisons fill by troop level, so a T8 march is left out first and displaced first, and its losses pay them points. T8 accounts stay home, shielded, and tap heals.

Six rules for everyone fighting

  • Reinforce with Saul (or Gordon, Howard, Fahd) in slot one; never Chenko, Amane, Amadeus or Jabel. The structure counts the four highest first skills inside.
  • Attack with Chenko or Amane in slot one, full march, join within the first minute.
  • Send only when your lead calls. An unasked march takes a seat from a better one.
  • Shield on except while your own attack march is out. Reinforcing keeps it. Only named leads' cities get garrisons; everyone else is protected by the shield and by having troops away.
  • Infirmary empty at 12:00, heal in batches with alliance help. Wounded beyond capacity die.
  • No city fights before 17:00 except the MOB strikers on an OPEN line. Afterwards rallies only, within three Town Center levels, never their six strongest.
