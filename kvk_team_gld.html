GLD: the castle

Your structure is the King's Castle of 2401. GLD takes it in the first minute after 12:00 UTC and holds it to 17:00. Hold it 2.5 hours without a break and the kingdom wins at once; if the hold breaks, the alliance with the most total castle time wins it for its kingdom. That is why every minute of castle time stays in GLD and only GLD members may send troops into it. Three heavy accounts join GLD for the day: Kogi, wh1t3p1xl and Livia_Lea.

Your alliance, your call. The goal is your structure. The roles and priorities below are a starting point, not orders. If your lead sees a better way to take and hold it, change the plan and tell the other leads what changed. Nobody outside the alliance needs to be asked.
