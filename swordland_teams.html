GLD Swordland: your group

Legion 1, first match, October 2026. Find your name, read your group's lines. The time is in alliance chat.

Before the match

  • One hour before: bring every march home, empty the infirmary, stop gathering. If troops are out or hurt, the game will not let you in.
  • Turn on your attack and defence boosts. Alliance territory bonuses do not work there; these do.
  • Heroes: garrison people put their best defence hero in slot 1. Strike puts the attack lineup in.
  • Thirty minutes before: open the battlefield chat. The team sheet is posted there again.
  • Your troops never die in Swordland. They get injured, and healing there is free.

The calls in chat

Only five words are used, each with a building name. Nothing else goes in battlefield chat during the hour.

DEFENDREINFORCERALLYCOLLECTROTATE

  • DEFEND Sanctum: your group takes that building now.
  • REINFORCE Sanctum: everyone with a spare march sends it there.
  • RALLY Swordshrine in 60 s, Bahaa first: join the rally when it appears.
  • COLLECT: a building flipped, looters go.
  • ROTATE: a Strike member leaves to heal, the named reserve steps in.

If your city is attacked

  • Send your troops out, to a tent or on a long march, before the hit lands. An empty city loses nothing.
  • If you burn out you are put back in the safe zone, healed, with nothing lost. Send a march out again.
  • Do not fight back alone against someone stronger. Tell the watcher; Strike deals with it.
