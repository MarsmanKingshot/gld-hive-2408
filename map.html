Move order

  1. Build the new banners first; they connect to today's territory and cover the block before anything moves.
  2. Rebuild the moving trap on its new tile right after its bear, so the cooldown covers the rebuild.
  3. Move the HQ last, once the block's banners stand on their own.
  4. Clear the two footprints of cities, then ring 1 (leads), then ring 2, then the rest. One teleport each.

Reading the map

North on this page is north in the game: up is plus X and plus Y together, right is plus X and minus Y. Every slot shows the X:Y to teleport to. A slot's coordinate is the tile the game reports for a city; check one existing city against its tile before the mail goes out.

Rally march times use the Kingshot Mapper's formula, 2.5 seconds per tile plus 4, centre to centre.
