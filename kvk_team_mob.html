Optional city team. Act only after leaders confirm current city-attack terms, the target and acceptable losses. An OPEN report is information, not permission to launch.

Observers and joiners. Report coordinates, shield status and time. Check the actual garrison and rally arrival; a departing march does not prove the city is undefended.

Protect your city. Keep a shield active whenever the game permits. Check its timer after teleporting. Read the warning before starting or joining an attack, scouting, or moving into the castle zone. Do not assume protection returns when troops come home: check any cooldown. Reinforcing was reported to preserve an existing shield; raising a new one with troops away still needs an in-game check.

Joiners

  • Send the troop tier, number and formation requested by your lead. T9/T10 is the proposed combat roster; other accounts can help with healing, observation or a specific skill assignment.
  • Suggested joiners: Saul or Gordon for defence; Chenko or Amane for attack. Check the first Expedition skill and follow the lead’s preset. These are choices, not the only valid heroes.
  • Rally leaders choose their full three-hero lineup. Joiner slot-one advice is not a rule for the leader’s lineup.
  • Send refills only when called. The lead checks capacity, the active garrison bonus provider and selected support skills. Do not assume four Sauls are always optimal.
  • Keep Infirmary space available and heal in manageable batches. Castle casualties overflow into deaths when it is full; later recovery depends on eligibility and Field Triage.
