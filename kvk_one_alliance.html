Castle and turret coordination

Proposal · reviewed 5 November 2026. Use this attack plan only if 2408 wins preparation. Confirm the result at 10:00 UTC Saturday. Each alliance confirms its own roster and any transfers; coordinate changes with the other leads.

Coordinating several alliances spreads the work of capturing, holding, refilling and healing. A single alliance can win; there is no rule requiring several. The practical question is whether the confirmed roster can cover the tasks.

Agree three things

  • Designate one castle-holding alliance to preserve cumulative occupation time.
  • Assign turrets and reserves from confirmed availability and battle reports, not alliance power alone.
  • Agree who refills each structure after a support team retakes it; members cannot directly reinforce another alliance’s garrison.

Enemy-held turrets fire increasingly often while held. Same-kingdom turrets do not fire on our castle and provide squad Lethality bonuses of 8%, 12%, 15% or 20% for one to four. Exact damage and firing intervals should be read in game.

Each alliance confirms its own roles. Support priority follows the situation and available troops; every turret team contributes.
