The design

Two bear traps side by side west of the HQ, 100 seats in three rings around them, the HQ stays where it is.

What needs to be cleared, rebuilt and taken down

All 13 banners for the hive are built. Every seat is on territory. The bannerwork can be viewed here: banner map. Green is built, red can be taken down after the move, purple banners are the paths out of the hive and stay.

Five resource tiles inside the hive have to be cleared with the Clearance Tool (Alliance Territory > Special Items) before the seats on them can be taken, each given by the lower-left tile of its 2 by 2. First the two on ring 1 seats: 742:459 (seat 2) and 746:479 (seat 17). Then 738:470 (seats 97 and 88), 736:477 (seat 58), 742:485 (seat 79). The tool only works on tiles nobody is gathering on; ask gatherers to recall first. Its blast radius is unknown: place the first one between 742:459 and 738:470 and see what it takes.

Bear trap 1 is to be rebuilt centred on 743:462 and bear trap 2 centred on 743:478; both sites are on territory. The game names a trap by its lower-left tile, so when placed correctly the trap panel will read 742:461 for trap 1 and 742:477 for trap 2. Rebuild each trap right after its own firing, the northern one (about 759:491) first, the cluster one (776:453) last. Before demolishing the first, read its panel for the rebuild cost and whether the Pitfall level and the timer survive.

Banners to take down: 753:459 and 754:481 are already gone. 746:451 can go now, nothing needs it. The 20 red banners over the old cluster and the northern cluster come down once the members and the alliance buildings under them have moved and both traps are rebuilt; taking them down earlier strips the Territory Bonus from the members still living there.

The map for members

We can point members to move to their location based on this map: https://marsmankingshot.github.io/gld-hive-2408/

Now these are generic names. Suggested rules for assigning seats:

  • Members choose their trap by the window they attend: Trap 1 at 12:00 UTC, Trap 2 at 22:00 UTC. 50 seats per trap.
  • Ring 1 (12 per trap): the rally leaders of that window, strongest rally first.
  • Ring 2 (18 at trap 1, 19 at trap 2): the strongest joiners, by power.
  • Ring 3 (20 at trap 1, 19 at trap 2): everyone else. The three seats beside the HQ ground (750:467, 750:469, 750:471) are the farthest, about 30 seconds to a trap; give them to farms or members who rarely join.
  • A seat belongs to a person. A newcomer takes the seat of the member who left.

Members can move now; every seat is on territory.

The Excel file

The names go into the Excel file: hive_workbook.xlsx. Open the sheet "3 Slots" and type each player's name in the yellow "Player" column of their seat; leave the other columns and the header row as they are and save it as .xlsx under the same name.

Then either upload it yourself at the project on GitHub (Marsman can add your GitHub account to the project), or send the file to Marsman and he uploads it. The hive plot is updated automatically (may take 2 minutes). This is convenient when people leave or join the alliance, or situations change.
