The map for members

We can point members to move to their location based on this map: https://marsmankingshot.github.io/gld-hive-2408/

Now these are generic names. Suggested rules for assigning seats:

  • Members choose their trap by the window they attend: Trap 1 at 12:00 UTC, Trap 2 at 22:00 UTC. 50 seats per trap.
  • Ring 1 (12 per trap): the rally leaders of that window, strongest rally first.
  • Ring 2 (18 at trap 1, 19 at trap 2): the strongest joiners, by power.
  • Ring 3 (20 at trap 1, 19 at trap 2): everyone else. The three seats beside the HQ ground (750:467, 750:469, 750:471) are the farthest, about 30 seconds to a trap; give them to farms or members who rarely join.
  • A seat belongs to a person. A newcomer takes the seat of the member who left.

Members can move now. 96 seats are fully on territory; the four beside the HQ ground follow when 752:477 stands. The workbook's sheet "3 Slots" has a "Wave" column: 0 means covered, 1 means waiting for 752:477.

The Excel file

The names go into the Excel file: hive_workbook.xlsx. Open the sheet "3 Slots" and type each player's name in the yellow "Player" column of their seat; leave the other columns and the header row as they are and save it as .xlsx under the same name.

Then either upload it yourself at the project on GitHub (Marsman can add your GitHub account to the project), or send the file to Marsman and he uploads it. The hive plot is updated automatically (may take 2 minutes). This is convenient when people leave or join the alliance, or situations change.
